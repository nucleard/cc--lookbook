# Coffee & Coconuts lookbook

A year of C&C visuals in 23 phone-first cards: the 2021 brandbook and the 2026 shelf side by side, then procedural treatments of the board, the wave and the sun in the brand's own colours.

Open `index.html` on a phone, or `_phone.html` on a desktop for a 390x844 frame. Swipe or use the arrow keys; `#n` jumps to card n.

Made by Daniel for Coffee & Coconuts. Logos, photography and product renders belong to Coffee & Coconuts.
